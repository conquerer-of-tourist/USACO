## Naming

Use `YYYY-MM-DD-coach-note-<problem-or-topic>.md`, with the review date in the America/Los_Angeles timezone.

Write coach notes in English by default.

## Review method

For future practice reviews:

1. Inspect the relevant repository changes, code, and student notes.
2. Read the relevant available ChatGPT conversations when assistance was used. Distinguish what appeared before a hint, what the hint supplied, and what was completed afterward.
3. Separate independent observations, guided implementation, AI-supplied code, and debugging help. Credit specific student contributions.
4. Keep judgments tied to visible evidence. If chat history or judge results are unavailable, state that limitation.
5. Distinguish local compilation/tests, a student's reported AC, independently verified judge results, and demonstrated understanding.
6. Use independent small-case or stress tests when needed to resolve a material correctness concern. Record what was actually tested.
7. Identify the most important remaining gap with a concrete example: modeling, proof, state meaning, implementation, testing, or independent recall.
8. Give a small follow-up checkpoint that tests explanation and reconstruction without the earlier solution.
9. Avoid unsupported percentage scores and conclusions about ability, effort, or motivation.
10. Save the review here and update the index.
